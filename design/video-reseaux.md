# Vidéo réseaux sociaux : plans Seedance et voix off

Format : vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920), environ 30 secondes.
Les plans Seedance servent **uniquement** à la vidéo des réseaux : l'aperçu App Store n'accepte que des images filmées dans l'appli.
Images de référence à joindre, dans `design/seedance-refs/`, dans l'ordre indiqué pour chaque plan (`@image1`, `@image2`…).

## Montage

| Temps | Image | Voix off / dialogue | Texte à l'écran |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Plan 1 : le capitaine et Pi | **Capitaine :** « Ohé, moussaillon ! Prêt à conquérir l'archipel ? » | — |
| 5–10 s | Plan 2 : le canon coule le Kraken | « Chaque bonne réponse charge ton canon. » | Bats les boss |
| 10–15 s | Extraits du jeu (tables, conjugaison) | « Tables, calcul, lecture, conjugaison, anglais : le programme du CP au CM2. » | Du CP au CM2 |
| 15–20 s | Plan 3 : le port se construit | « Gagne des trésors et bâtis ton port. » | Construis ton port |
| 20–25 s | Plan 4 : l'île sort de la brume | « Quinze îles t'attendent. » | 15 îles à découvrir |
| 25–30 s | Écran final (visuel story) | « Moussaillon, l'école pirate. Gratuit, sans pub, sans achat. » | Gratuit · sans pub · sans achat — App Store et web |

Voix off : une voix chaleureuse et complice, qui s'adresse aux enfants comme à leurs parents. Le capitaine a une voix grave et joviale.

## Plan 1 — Le capitaine se présente
Références : `@image1` = guide-capitaine.png, `@image2` = bg-port-dusk.png

```
SCENE CONTEXT
A jovial old pirate captain stands on a wooden dock at golden sunset, his green parrot on his left shoulder, and greets the viewer directly with one cheerful line.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@image1: elderly pirate captain, stocky build, long braided white beard, black tricorne hat with red feather and gold trim, navy coat with gold buttons, red sash, green-red-blue parrot perched on his left shoulder; warm, friendly, energetic; deep jovial voice. 100% matches the reference.
@image2: wooden harbour dock at sunset with lanterns, barrels and a red-sailed ship moored in the background. 100% matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: the captain from mid-thigh up, centered. Midground: dock planks, two barrels, a lit lantern on a post to frame right. Background: calm sea, red-sailed ship at anchor, sun low on the horizon to frame left. Camera on the dock at the captain's chest height, sun behind him to the left.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Captain already mid-gesture: right hand raised in a wave, eyes on the lens, smiling; the parrot bobs its head on his shoulder. Vertical framing, captain filling the lower two thirds, hat tip clear of the top edge.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, the camera does not cut on its own.

CAMERA
Slow push-in from medium shot to medium close-up at 1 km/h, eye-level, gentle handheld float, focus locked on the captain's eyes.

OPTICS
47° neutral perspective easing to 29° portrait compression by the end, soft background separation.

ACTION
0–1 s: the captain finishes his wave and leans 10 cm toward the lens. 1–4 s: he says the line, eyebrows lifting, beard bouncing with each word. 4–5 s: he winks; the parrot spreads both wings wide and squawks once.

PERFORMANCE
Big warm smile reaching the eyes, cheeks lifting, crow's-feet creasing; lively catch-lights in both eyes; the parrot's head tracks the captain's face.

PHYSICS
Beard braids and the hat feather sway with each head movement; the coat fabric creases at the elbow; the parrot's claws grip the shoulder pad as it flaps.

LIGHTING
Warm low sun at 3200K backlighting from frame left, golden rim light on the beard and hat; soft warm fill from the lantern at frame right; gentle lens glow around the sun.

AUDIO
Captain, in French, cheerful and booming: "Ohé, moussaillon ! Prêt à conquérir l'archipel ?" Then one parrot squawk. Soft harbour ambience, small waves, a distant gull.

STYLE
Painterly stylized 3D animation, warm saturated colours, rounded friendly shapes, rich hand-painted textures, family-friendly adventure tone, vertical 9:16.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The parrot stays on the captain's left shoulder for the whole shot. Red feather and gold trim on the hat stay visible. Captain faces the lens throughout.
```

## Plan 2 — Le canon coule le Kraken
Références : `@image1` = ship-player-3.png, `@image2` = boss-kraken.png, `@image3` = bg-storm.png

```
SCENE CONTEXT
On a stormy sea, the hero's red-sailed pirate ship fires one cannon shot at a giant cartoon kraken wearing a pirate hat; the cannonball hits and the kraken sinks in a burst of water and sparkles.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@image1: three-masted wooden pirate ship with red sails, gold trim and a black skull flag on the main mast. 100% matches the reference.
@image2: giant purple kraken with big friendly cartoon eyes, black pirate tricorne with gold wheel emblem and red bandana, curling tentacles with orange suckers. 100% matches the reference.
@image3: purple-orange storm sky over a turquoise sea, lightning on the horizon, rain. 100% matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: the ship at frame left, bow pointing right, its side cannons facing the kraken. Midground: the kraken rising from the waves at frame right, three times as tall as the ship's mast. Background: storm clouds, lightning far right. Camera low above the water between them, slightly behind the ship.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Ship and kraken both in frame, the kraken's tentacles raised high, rain falling diagonally; a cannon port on the ship already glowing orange.

FORMAT MODE
Sequence of cuts, no timecodes; cuts only at the specified points, the camera does not cut on its own.
CUT 1 — wide: the ship's cannon fires with a burst of orange flame and white smoke; the cannonball arcs across the sky toward the kraken.
CUT 2 — medium on the kraken: the cannonball strikes its hat; a bright splash of golden sparkles; the kraken's eyes go wide in a comic surprised look.
CUT 3 — wide: the kraken sinks backward into a tall spray of white water, the hat spinning on the surface; the ship rocks on the wave.

CAMERA
CUT 1: 84° wide, low over the waves, slow drift right following the cannonball at 15 km/h. CUT 2: 29°, static, eye-level with the kraken. CUT 3: 63°, slight push-in toward the spray. No drift mid-segment beyond the stated moves.

PHYSICS
The cannon recoils 30 cm on firing; smoke rolls and thins in the wind; the cannonball follows a clean parabola; the spray rises to mast height and falls back as droplets; waves lift and drop the hull.

LIGHTING
Cool storm light at 6500K from above, one lightning flash at the moment of impact lighting everything white-blue for a few frames; warm orange glow from the cannon fire.

AUDIO
Cannon boom, whistling cannonball, splash and sparkle chime, cartoon gulp from the kraken, rain and thunder.

STYLE
Painterly stylized 3D animation, rich saturated colours, comic and family-friendly, no gore, vertical 9:16.

POSITIVE LOCKS
Red sails and skull flag stay visible in CUT 1 and CUT 3. The kraken keeps its pirate hat and red bandana until the hat floats away in CUT 3. The kraken looks surprised and cartoonish, always friendly.
```

## Plan 3 — Le port se construit, avec sa statue
Références : `@image1` = port-base.png, `@image2` = bld-statue.png

```
SCENE CONTEXT
High above a small tropical harbour island, empty sandy building plots fill up one after another with colourful houses, a market and a lighthouse, and finally a golden statue of a young pirate rises on its pedestal in the central square.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@image1: small isometric tropical harbour island seen from above, sandy plots, wooden pier, palm trees, turquoise water. 100% matches the reference.
@image2: golden statue of a young pirate holding a spyglass and a sword, on a stone pedestal with a gold ship's wheel emblem and red flowers around it. 100% matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
The island centered, seen from a high three-quarter angle; the pier at bottom right; the central square in the middle of the island. Camera high above the water to the south, sun from the upper left.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
The island with all plots empty and sandy, the central square bare, small waves around the shore.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, the camera does not cut on its own.

CAMERA
Slow orbit of 40° clockwise around the island at 3 km/h while descending slightly, ending closer on the central square. 63° wide, easing to 47° at the end.

ACTION
0–3 s: buildings pop up on the plots one by one with a playful bounce, each with a puff of golden sparkles: red-roof houses, a striped market stall, a small lighthouse. 3–5 s: in the central square the golden statue rises from the ground onto its pedestal, gleaming, flowers blooming around the base.

PHYSICS
Each building overshoots 10% in height then settles; sparkles drift up and fade; palm leaves sway in a light breeze; small waves roll against the pier.

LIGHTING
Bright late-afternoon sun at 5600K from the upper left, warm highlights on the roofs, a strong golden glint travelling across the statue as it settles.

AUDIO
Cheerful wooden building pops, a chime for each building, a triumphant brass hit when the statue lands, gentle surf.

STYLE
Painterly stylized 3D isometric animation, warm saturated colours, toy-like charm, vertical 9:16.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The island shape and pier stay identical through the orbit. The statue is gold with a spyglass and a sword, on a stone pedestal with a ship's wheel emblem.
```

## Plan 4 — L'île sort de la brume
Références : `@image1` = isle-horloge.png, `@image2` = ship-player-3.png

```
SCENE CONTEXT
At dawn, a red-sailed pirate ship glides toward a thick bank of mist; the mist parts and reveals a small tropical island crowned by a clock tower, its red flag unfurling.

ACTIVE REFERENCES
@image1: small round tropical island with a stone clock tower with a large white clock face and a red flag, palm trees and red flowers. 100% matches the reference.
@image2: three-masted wooden pirate ship with red sails and gold trim. 100% matches the reference.

LOCATION MAP
Foreground: the ship's stern at frame bottom left, sailing away from camera. Midground: a wall of white mist across the frame. Background: the island hidden inside the mist, centered. Camera low behind the ship, sun rising behind the island.

FIRST FRAME / BLOCKING
Mist density 90% across the midground, only a faint silhouette of the clock tower visible; the ship already moving toward it.

FORMAT MODE
One continuous shot, the camera does not cut on its own.

CAMERA
Slow follow behind the ship at 8 km/h, then a gentle crane up of 3 m as the island appears. 47° neutral perspective throughout.

ACTION
0–2 s: the ship sails into the mist edge. 2–4 s: the mist thins from 90% to 20% from the centre outward, revealing the island and its clock tower; the clock hands turn one full hour. 4–5 s: the red flag unfurls on top of the tower; sun rays break through behind the island.

PHYSICS
Mist rolls and curls around the masts as the ship passes; the bow cuts a white wake; light rays become visible in the thinning mist; the flag ripples in the wind.

LIGHTING
Soft dawn light at 4000K from behind the island, golden god-rays through the mist, cool blue shadows on the water in the foreground.

AUDIO
Soft wind and waves, creaking ship timber, a deep clock chime as the island appears, a gentle magical shimmer.

STYLE
Painterly stylized 3D animation, misty pastel dawn turning golden, sense of discovery, vertical 9:16.

POSITIVE LOCKS
The island keeps its clock tower with a white clock face and red flag. The ship keeps its red sails and stays in the lower left third of the frame.
```

## Remarques
- Seedance génère le dialogue du plan 1 ; les autres phrases de voix off sont à enregistrer à part, avec un générateur de voix ou au micro, puis à poser au montage.
- Les extraits du jeu (10–15 s) peuvent venir de `design/com/apercu-app-store-886x1920.mp4`.
- Gardez la même voix pour toute la voix off, et une musique du jeu en fond (`assets/audio/mus-escale.mp3` ou `mus-boss.mp3`).
